Score Entry

One game of a match report: a compact replay link plus analyze in the header, an overridable winner and score, and a roster per side where each Pokémon carries a status dropdown and three boxed kill steppers. The score tracks survivors until someone types over it, then a reset button hands it back. Nothing is flagged while you type — roster gaps, unaccounted KOs and missing winners are collected by `scoreEntryWarnings` and raised once, in a dialog, on submit. Shared by the league coach report and the draft opponent score page.

Score Entry — Match Header

The match-level row above the games: a team link and an overridable series score per side, a forfeit toggle that hands the win to the other team and takes the games out of play, and a winner select that follows the score until it is set by hand. A forfeit captions the select to say why it locked. Pass a notes control and it relabels itself to a required explanation the moment a forfeit is toggled on. Shared by the league coach report and the draft opponent score page.

Score Entry — Submit Checks

What `scoreEntryWarnings` collects while you fill a report, raised once on submit instead of nagging inline. Grouped by where each problem came from, and never blocking — "Submit anyway" is a real option, because a coach can be right and the reconciliation wrong.
